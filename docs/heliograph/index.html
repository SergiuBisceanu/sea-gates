Field manual

How to play

Light enters from the west. Guide it through the instruments and out into the eastern channels where ships are approaching.

Rotate
Tap an instrument to turn it. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move and Enter or Space to turn. Each turn costs one order. Undo gives it back.
Split
Light enters at strength 4. A mirror turns the whole beam. A prism sends half straight ahead and reflects half. Watch which channels light up.
Defend
Read the fleet forecast before firing. A ship that receives too little light reaches the harbor and damages its hull. Survive seven tides to win.
Oracle
Ask the Oracle for a move when you need help tracing a route. You still choose which instruments to turn.
